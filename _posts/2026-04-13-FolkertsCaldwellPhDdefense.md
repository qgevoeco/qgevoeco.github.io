[Molly Folkerts Caldwell](/people) presented and defended her PhD dissertation research today! Way to go Dr. Caldwell!

Molly spear-headed our Auburn Urban Turtle Ecology projects, mostly focusing on yellow-bellied sliders (_Trachemys scripta scripta_). Molly defended with two chapters published and another on its way!

We hope Dr. Caldwell can stick around and share her amazing southeastern natural history knowledge with Auburn undergrads as a lecturer in the Biological Sciences Department. And of course, continue to come out turtleing with the Auburn Turtle Crew!
